Who We Are

About Sri Ram Traders

Your Trusted Construction Partner

Sri Ram Traders is an authorized wholesale stockist and premium builders supplier based in Gangapatti, Bihar. With over a decade of commitment to excellence, we supply high-grade building materials—including TMT steel rods, aggregates (giti), premium red bricks, and high-strength cement—directly from top-tier quarries and manufacturers.

We ensure transparent weight check (Dharam Kanta) verifications and prompt doorstep site unloading with our advanced hydraulic dumper fleet. From small-scale home builds to mega infrastructure projects, we are dedicated to driving your project's structural integrity forward.
